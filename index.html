Why Clean Your Air Ducts & Dryer Vents in Georgia?

Atlanta's high humidity and heavy seasonal pollen get pulled directly into your HVAC system. Cleaning your ductwork removes circulating allergens, improves cooling efficiency, and eliminates musty odors.

1. Allergy & Pollen Relief

Extract trapped Georgia pine pollen, dust mites, and pet dander so your family breathes cleaner indoor air.

2. Lower Energy Bills

Unclogged ducts and clean blower fans allow your AC unit to cool your home faster with less strain.

3. Fire Hazard Prevention

Clogged dryer vents and creosote-filled chimneys are leading causes of residential fires. We clear them safely.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the $99 Basic Air Duct Cleaning promo?

The $99 promo covers basic negative-pressure air duct cleaning for 1 AC unit, including supply and return vents and a free visual inspection of your system.

How long does air duct and dryer vent cleaning take?

Most single-system homes in Atlanta take between 60 and 120 minutes depending on the number of vents and whether deep brush cleaning or dryer vent service is added.

Will dust get into my house during the cleaning?

No. Our negative-pressure HEPA vacuum system keeps the entire ductwork under suction so all dust and debris travel directly into our containment unit.

How often should dryer vents be cleaned?

Fire safety experts recommend cleaning your dryer vent at least once every 12 months, or sooner if clothes take longer than one cycle to dry.
